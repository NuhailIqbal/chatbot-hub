# Demo Support Knowledge Base — Acme Helpdesk (sample data)

This is the sample knowledge base loaded by the ingestion pipeline (the `Edit Fields1` node). It is fictional data, written for the demo — replace it with your own product docs.

## 1. Product summary

Acme Helpdesk is a fictional customer-support platform used in this demo. It helps support teams answer customer questions, track tickets, and follow up on unresolved chats.

## 2. Plans and pricing

- Starter: $29/month, 1 workspace, 500 tickets/month.
- Growth: $99/month, 5 workspaces, 5,000 tickets/month, priority support.
- Business: custom pricing, unlimited workspaces, SLA and a dedicated manager.
- All plans include a 14-day free trial. No credit card is required to start a trial.

## 3. Getting started

Sign up with a work email, create your first workspace, invite your team, and connect one support channel (email, web chat, or WhatsApp). Setup takes under 10 minutes.

## 4. Tickets

Tickets can be opened by email, chat, or the web form. Each ticket has a status (open, pending, resolved), a priority (low, medium, high, urgent), and an owner. Urgent tickets are escalated to the on-call agent automatically.

## 5. Support hours

Live chat support runs Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00 PKT. Email support is answered within one business day. The help center is available 24/7.

## 6. Integrations

Acme Helpdesk connects to Slack, HubSpot, and Zendesk. The Slack integration posts a message whenever an urgent ticket is created. The HubSpot integration syncs ticket status back to the contact record.

## 7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Workspaces are isolated per team. Admins can enable two-factor authentication for all agents.
